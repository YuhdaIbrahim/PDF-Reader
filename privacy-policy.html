Privacy Policy for PDF Reader & Scanner: Editor

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what the app PDF Reader & Scanner: Editor ("the app") does with your information. It is published by 4K Anime Wallpapers Dev ("we"). Contact: developerx168@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The app works on your device. Your documents are not uploaded to us or to any server by the app.
  • We have no accounts. The app uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to count which features are used and to find crashes (see "Analytics and crash reporting" below). This data never includes your documents.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. These ads may use your device's advertising ID (see "Advertising" below). A Premium subscription removes all ads.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we never receive your documents.

What the app can access, and why

Access Why it is needed
All files access (Android "Manage all files") or storage read permission on older Android To find the PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files already on your device and show them in one list. The app reads a file only when you open it or choose a tool for it.
Camera To scan documents and ID cards, and to capture text for text recognition. The camera is used only while a scan screen is open. Photos are processed on your device.
Photos you pick When you choose images (for example for "Image to PDF" or "Image to Text"), the app reads only the images you select.

The app writes the files you create (scans, converted, merged, edited or protected copies) into your device's Documents/PDF Scanner and Pictures/PDF Scanner folders. Your original files are not changed unless you rename, move or delete them from within the app.

What stays on your device

  • Recent files, bookmarks and your sort choice are saved as small preferences in the app's private storage. They contain file references, not file contents.
  • For a password-protected PDF you open, the app creates a temporary unlocked copy in its private cache so it can be shown. The password you type is not stored, and these temporary copies are deleted the next time the app starts.
  • Text recognition ("Image to Text", scanned PDF to Word) runs on the device using a recognition model bundled in the app.

Third-party components

  • Google ML Kit Document Scanner (used by "Scan to PDF"). It runs through Google Play services on your device. Google may process limited technical data as described in the ML Kit terms and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your scans.

  • Open-source libraries for PDF handling, camera access and the user interface. They run on your device.

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob): shows banner, full-screen and app-open ads in the free version (see below).

  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google): usage statistics and crash reports (see below).
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): shows a consent form where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK).
  • Google Play Billing: handles the Premium subscription. Payment is processed by Google Play; we never see your card details. We receive only whether your subscription is active.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. They may collect:

  • Usage events, such as which tool you open (for example "Merge PDF"), app opens, and whether a Premium purchase was completed. Events do not include file names or file contents.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, Android version, language, country, app version and a randomly generated app instance ID (and the advertising ID where you have allowed it).
  • Crash reports, such as the crash stack trace and the state of the app when it crashed.

We use this data only to improve the app and to measure our advertising of the app. It is not used to identify you personally, and we do not sell it. Google processes it under the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy. In the EEA, UK and other regions where required, analytics follows your consent choice in the consent form.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the main screen, a full-screen ad with every second "Saved successfully" screen, and an ad while the app opens. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions, and may use it to personalise ads. Your documents and their contents are never sent to ad networks.

  • In the EEA, UK and other regions where required, you are asked for consent the first time you open the app. You can change your choice later where the consent form offers it.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • See How Google uses data and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • With an active Premium subscription the app does not request ads.

Subscriptions

Premium is a recurring subscription (monthly or yearly, with a free trial for new subscribers where offered) bought through Google Play. Manage or cancel it in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions. The app keeps a small local flag that Premium is active so it works offline. Purchases are linked to your Google account by Google, not by us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Files are handled on your device under Android's normal app protections. Please note: the "Edit Text" tool covers old text with a new layer; the old text can still exist in the file. Do not use it to remove confidential information.

Your choices

You can revoke the camera or files permission in your device settings at any time (some features will then stop working). Uninstalling the app removes its private data. Files you created remain in your Documents and Pictures folders until you delete them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be described in the app's store listing or release notes.
